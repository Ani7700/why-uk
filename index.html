Why the UK?

Beyond my professional ambitions, there is also a deeply personal reason why I want to build my career in the UK. Someone incredibly important to me, whom I love deeply, is based in London. For me, working in the UK is not simply a career decision; it is also about building a life in a place where I can grow professionally while being close to the person who means the most to me.

This is also why I chose to apply for jobs in London after completing my internship and receiving a return offer in Tokyo. I know that finding a job in the UK as a recent graduate is extremely difficult, but I am willing to be brave for the person I love.

I am looking for a team where I can bring my curiosity, technical skills and willingness to learn, take ownership of meaningful problems and grow alongside people who are passionate about what they do.

I genuinely hope to build a long-term future in the UK, both in my career and in my personal life.

I would truly appreciate this opportunity and be grateful for any chance I am given.

Ninghan Qi
